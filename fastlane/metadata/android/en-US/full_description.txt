Cheap Fuel shows the cheapest petrol stations within the radius you choose (1 to 50 km) around your location, using the official prices from Spain's Ministry for the Ecological Transition.

• Petrol 95 and 98, diesel, premium diesel and LPG.
• List sorted by price or distance with the cheapest in green, or a map with the price on every station.
• Favourites on top, an "open now" filter and a label for 24-hour stations.
• Tapping a station opens the maps app you choose.
• "3 cheapest" widget.

No account, no ads, no tracking. Your location is only used on the phone to work out distances and is never sent anywhere. Without a connection it uses the last saved prices and shows their date. Free software (GPL-3.0).
